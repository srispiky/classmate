Phase 2 – Sprint 5

Chunk 0B – Audit Field Remediation (AB-002)

# Goal

Complete audit-readiness remediation identified during Sprint 4 Readiness Review.

This is an architecture hardening task.

Do not implement new business features.

Do not modify authorization behavior.

Do not modify OpenAPI.

Focus exclusively on audit field consistency.

# Background

Sprint 4 Readiness Review identified missing audit fields.

Current standards require all mutable business entities to support:

* created_at
* updated_at
* created_by
* updated_by

Soft deletable entities should also support:

* deleted_at
* deleted_by

# Target Standard

Every mutable business entity must support:

```sql
created_at timestamptz not null
updated_at timestamptz not null

created_by uuid null
updated_by uuid null
```

Follow existing project conventions.

# Scope

Review all business entities including:

* courses
* assignments
* assessments
* notes
* announcements
* course_enrollments

Review any additional mutable entities that exist.
