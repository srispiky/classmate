Phase 2 – Sprint 5

Chunk 0B Review and Closure

Objective

Audit and validate the completed implementation of AB-002 Audit Field Remediation before authorizing additional Sprint 5 work.

Do not implement new functionality.

Perform an architectural review only.

Background

Chunk 0B introduced audit consistency improvements across mutable business entities.

Required audit fields:

* created_at
* updated_at
* created_by
* updated_by

Soft deletable entities additionally require:

* deleted_at
* deleted_by

Project standards require:

* PostgreSQL-first conventions
* snake_case columns
* timestamptz timestamps
* UTC only
* service-driven audit population
* repository persistence only
