## PART 2 — RISK LEVEL

Create enum:

LOW
MEDIUM
HIGH
INSUFFICIENT_DATA

Rules:

fewer than 3 scored events
→ INSUFFICIENT_DATA

average < 60
→ HIGH

60–79
→ MEDIUM

80+
→ LOW

---

## PART 3 — TREND

Create enum:

IMPROVING
STABLE
DECLINING
INSUFFICIENT_DATA

Rules:

fewer than 5 scored events
→ INSUFFICIENT_DATA

Use most recent scored events only.

Compare:

average(previous 2)
vs
average(last 3)

delta > +5
→ IMPROVING

delta < -5
→ DECLINING

otherwise
→ STABLE
