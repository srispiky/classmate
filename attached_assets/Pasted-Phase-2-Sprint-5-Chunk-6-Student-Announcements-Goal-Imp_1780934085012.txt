# Endpoints

Implement:

## List Announcements

GET /student/announcements

Returns announcements visible to the authenticated student.

## Announcement Detail

GET /student/announcements/{announcementId}

Returns announcement details.

Access must be denied when:

* announcement does not exist
* announcement belongs to a non-enrolled course
* announcement is soft deleted
